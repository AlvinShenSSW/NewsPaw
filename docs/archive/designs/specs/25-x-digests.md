# X03 / #25 — X account and interest-group editions

Closes #25

A special account has an independent edition with its own time, Bot assignments and style. Ordinary accounts belong to one X subscription. A special account only joins that subscription's aggregate when explicitly opted in. Moving to another subscription or partition clears the previous opt-in. Existing accounts retain their original configuration and default special accounts remain separate. Normal and adult subscriptions, provider profiles, Telegram targets and cached post bodies remain partitioned.

The existing worker takes one official account timeline page per advance, up to two pages of ten rows per refresh. Default digest input uses original and quote posts. Jobs in an exact compatible account scope share pending managed scans and complete scans up to an hour old. Each job and account group remembers its scan, with one durable paid step per page. Before creating a paid intent, the worker checks the current X binding, job lease, subscription and schedule. Unknown attempts are held. Incomplete scans do not advance the last complete cursor.

The editorial system receives typed X evidence with author, stable post link, explicit publication time and observed time where recorded. Quoted references do not add a network read or corroborate claims. Attributed output shows whose account posted the material. Coverage records its actual start/end and partial or unavailable status, including reused boundaries. An empty result with complete coverage is distinguished from a failed or unverified empty result. A removed or revoked post is checked before another model call or delivery. Separate authorized editions may share one collected post.

Tests cover route overrides, normal/adult partition changes, exact evidence and Bot scope, concurrent shared reads, known page recovery, budget intent before dispatch, partial and empty coverage, current access and source attribution. Verification uses fake X and model transports. No credentials or live sends are used.
